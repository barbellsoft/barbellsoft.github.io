About Barbellsoft

Barbellsoft is one person: a developer who lifts.

The name comes from the gym. When a heavy lift goes wrong, a spotter grabs the bar. These tools are meant for the same moment on a computer: a USB drive that will not open, a file you cannot find.

Every tool here is free. There are no ads and no tracking, on this site or in the programs.
