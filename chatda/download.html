Easiest way on a Mac, no security warning:

  1. Press Command (⌘) + Space to open Spotlight, type Terminal and press Return.
  2. Click Copy below, click in the Terminal window, press Command (⌘) + V to paste, then press Return.
  3. Wait about a minute. Chatda is installed in Applications and opens by itself.
curl -fsSL https://github.com/barbellsoft/chatda-releases/releases/latest/download/install.sh | bash

If Windows warns you

Chatda is not code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may say it does not recognize the app. Check that the file is the one published here by comparing its SHA-256 in PowerShell:

Get-FileHash .\Chatda_x64-setup.exe -Algorithm SHA256

If the value matches, choose More info, then Run anyway. Installing and updating ask for administrator approval because Chatda installs for all users together with its drive indexing service. On a Mac, if it says Chatda can't be opened, click Done, open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway, then open Chatda again.

A code-signing certificate, about US$200 a year, makes this warning much rarer. Help with a donation
