Compress an image to a target size: 20 KB, 50 KB, 100 KB or more

Choose a photo and a size limit. The tool tries different quality levels and dimensions until the file fits, then shows the result.

Save the compressed file

How it works

  • The tool saves the photo again at several quality levels and keeps the highest quality that fits the limit. If even medium quality is too large, it makes the picture smaller and tries again.
  • Here 1 KB is 1,000 bytes, so the result also stays under the limit on sites that count 1 KB as 1,024 bytes.
  • Photo information such as camera, date and location (EXIF) is not kept in the new file. Transparent areas become white in JPG.
  • Very large photos and some formats such as HEIC may not open, depending on the browser.

This tool does not send the text or files you enter.
