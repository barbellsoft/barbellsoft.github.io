All of our tools are free, with no ads and no tracking. If ReviveUSB brought your photos back or Chatda found the file you needed, a coffee keeps these tools going. Donations are optional and unlock nothing.

Where your coffee goes

  • A code-signing certificate, about US$200 a year. Right now Windows shows an "unrecognized app" warning before ReviveUSB runs. With a certificate, that warning goes away for everyone who downloads it.
  • Drives to test on. ReviveUSB has been tested on real drives, but only a few brands. USB sticks and SD cards from other makers, broken ones included, let me test against the failures people actually have.
  • Keeping this site and the downloads online, in 11 languages.

In the gym, a spotter catches the bar when a lift goes wrong. Your coffee does the same for the next person whose drive suddenly asks to be formatted.
