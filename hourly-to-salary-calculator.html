How it is calculated

  • Yearly = hourly × hours per week × weeks per year. Monthly = yearly ÷ 12. Weekly = hourly × hours per week.
  • All amounts are before tax. Taxes, social insurance, overtime and bonuses are not included.
  • 52 weeks is a full year. If some weeks are unpaid, such as unpaid leave, enter fewer weeks.
  • Enter the amount in the currency you are paid in. The tool does not convert currencies.
