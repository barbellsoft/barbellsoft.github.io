Barbell Launcher help

How to use

  • Press Alt+Space anywhere. The search box opens at the top of the screen.
  • Type a few letters and press Enter. The first line is already selected.
  • Use ↑ and ↓ to choose another line, and Esc to close.

What you can type

  • An app name or part of it: ‘excel’, ‘vsc’ for Visual Studio Code. Korean initial consonants (ㅋㅋㅇ) and text typed in the wrong Korean/English input mode also work.
  • A calculation: ‘123+5’, ‘1,500,0003.3%’. Enter copies the result.
  • Korean office math: ‘부가세 110000’, ‘3.3% 1000000’, ‘30평’, ‘1234500’ (amount in Korean words).
  • Dates: ‘today+30’, ‘+10bd’ (working days, skipping Korean public holidays), ‘12/31’ (D-day, month/day), ‘11/1~12/31’.
  • Web: ‘g words’ searches Google, ‘n words’ searches Naver, ‘example.com’ opens the site.
  • Commands: lock, sleep, settings, shut down, restart, sign out. Shut down, restart and sign out need Enter twice.

Shortcuts and snippets

  • Open ‘Manage shortcuts’ from the tray icon. Give an address, a folder or a text a keyword, then type the keyword.
  • Put {검색어} in an address to search with the words after the keyword.
  • Enter on a snippet pastes it where you were typing.
  • Export a file to share shortcuts with your team. Snippets are left out unless you choose them.
  • Snippets are saved on this PC without encryption. Don't save passwords.

Your data stays on this PC

  • What you type in the search box is not saved.
  • Barbell Launcher saves only how often you start each app, your settings, and your shortcuts.
  • It does not connect to the internet by itself. Only a web search line opens your browser.

When it does not work

  • If the shortcut does not open the box, another program may use it. Choose another one in Settings.
  • A snippet can't be pasted into a window that runs as administrator. It is on the clipboard, so press Ctrl+V.
