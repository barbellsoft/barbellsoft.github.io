PasteFit

Pasted text comes out with broken lines and odd fonts? One shortcut makes it fit.

  • Press Win+Alt+V where you want to paste: a preview shows the result, and Enter pastes it
  • Fixes the broken lines you get when you copy from a PDF, and keeps paragraphs and lists
  • Keep text only: drops fonts, colors and links, and cleans up stray spaces
  • Make one line: for a search box, an input field or a single cell
  • Pastes tables from web pages or Excel as clean tables in Word, Outlook and browsers, and as cells in Excel
  • Picks the right way for you and shows exactly what it changed
  • Reads the clipboard only when you press the shortcut: no history, nothing saved, nothing sent over the internet
  • Waits in the notification area, one exe file, no installation (about 8 MB)
  • Available in 11 languages
  • and more...
