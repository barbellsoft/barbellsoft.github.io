Privacy

This site does not collect personal information on its own. It counts visits with GoatCounter, which uses no cookies and does not store IP addresses: it records only the page, the site you came from, your browser, screen size and country.

The feedback form sends what you write, and your email only if you enter it, to our inbox through FormSubmit. We use it only to read and answer your message.

Your theme choice is stored only in your own browser (localStorage) and never leaves it.

Donation buttons lead to third-party sites, which have their own privacy policies.

The programs do not send your files or personal information to us or anyone else. Chatda checks GitHub for new versions and connects only to network folders or servers you set up yourself. ReviveUSB saves its language choice in a small settings file on your PC, and its Check for updates menu just opens this site in your browser. Barbell Launcher saves only how often you start each app, its settings and the shortcuts you add, in a file on your PC. It does not save what you type in the search box and does not connect to the internet by itself.

ReviveUSB Pro is the one exception. When you enter a license key, ReviveUSB sends the key and your PC's name to Lemon Squeezy, our reseller, to activate it, and checks the key again when the program starts. The key is saved only in a settings file on your PC, and your files are never sent. When you buy, Lemon Squeezy collects your email and payment details under its own privacy policy and shares your email and order with us so we can answer support requests.

Ads on this website

This website may show ads from Google AdSense. The programs themselves never show ads. Pages with download buttons are kept free of ads.

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your earlier visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies lets it and its partners serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and other sites on the internet.

You can turn off personalized ads in Google's Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some other vendors' use of cookies for personalized ads at aboutads.info.

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are asked for their choice through Google's consent message before ads use cookies for personalization.
