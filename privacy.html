Privacy

This site has no cookies and does not collect personal information on its own. It counts visits with GoatCounter, which uses no cookies and does not store IP addresses: it records only the page, the site you came from, your browser, screen size and country.

The feedback form sends what you write, and your email only if you enter it, to our inbox through FormSubmit. We use it only to read and answer your message.

Your theme choice is stored only in your own browser (localStorage) and never leaves it.

Donation buttons lead to third-party sites, which have their own privacy policies.

The programs do not send your files or personal information to us or anyone else. Chatda checks GitHub for new versions and connects only to network folders or servers you set up yourself. ReviveUSB saves its language choice in a small settings file on your PC, and its Check for updates menu just opens this site in your browser.
