Refund policy

Try the free version of ReviveUSB before you buy. It tells you how many files it found and saves up to 500 MB of them, so you can check that your files can be recovered before you pay.

We give a full refund within 14 days of purchase if ReviveUSB Pro could not save files that the free version had found, if the license key could not be activated, or if you were charged twice. Reply to your purchase receipt email, or write to softbarbell@gmail.com with your order number and a short description of the problem. If you can, attach the ReviveUSB-report file that ReviveUSB saves in your recovery folder.

Because you can check the result for free first, we do not give refunds after a recovery has completed normally, or for a change of mind. This does not limit any rights you have under the consumer protection laws of your country.

Refunds are processed by Lemon Squeezy, our reseller, and paid back to the original payment method. When a refund is made, the license key is deactivated.
