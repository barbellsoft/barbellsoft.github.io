ReviveUSB

Broken USB? Get your files back for free, and fix the drive.

A USB drive often holds things you can't make again, like family photos or months of work. I built ReviveUSB to treat your files as carefully as I'd treat my own, so that as much as possible comes back.

Watch the 1-minute how-to video (YouTube)

  • Only reads your drive while backing up and recovering
  • Copies the whole drive to an image first, and resumes if interrupted
  • Brings files back with their names and folders
  • NTFS, FAT32 and exFAT: current and deleted files, even when Windows won't open the drive
  • Finds photos, videos (MP4, MOV), PDFs and ZIP files by signature when names are gone
  • Check, format or rebuild the drive, then test it and copy the recovered files back
  • One exe file, no installation (about 8 MB)
  • Available in 11 languages
  • and more...
