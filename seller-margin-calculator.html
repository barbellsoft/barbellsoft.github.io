How it is calculated

  • Fee = price × fee rate. Profit = price − fee − cost − shipping − other costs. Margin = profit ÷ price.
  • Price needed = (cost + shipping + other costs) ÷ (1 − fee rate − target margin). If the fee rate and target margin add up to 100% or more, no price can reach it.
  • Taxes such as VAT or income tax are not included. Check them with your accountant.
