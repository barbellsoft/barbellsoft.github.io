How it counts and cleans

  • Clean up removes spaces at the start and end of each line, turns runs of spaces into one space and keeps one blank line between paragraphs.
  • When lines are joined, a word split by a hyphen at the end of a line is put back together, and Chinese and Japanese lines are joined without a space.
  • A line break counts as one character in “Characters” and is not counted in “Characters without spaces”.
  • Bytes are counted in UTF-8. Forms that count in an older encoding can show a different number.

This tool does not send the text or files you enter.
