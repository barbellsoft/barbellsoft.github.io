Free tools that work in your browser

Each tool works in your browser. The tools do not send the text or files you enter.

  • Character counter
    Count characters with and without spaces, words, lines and bytes. Remove extra spaces and blank lines, or join lines that broke when you copied from a PDF.
  • Seller margin calculator
    Enter your price, product cost, marketplace fee and shipping to see your payout, profit and margin, and the price you need for a target margin.
  • Hourly to salary calculator
    Convert an hourly wage to weekly, monthly and yearly pay, or a salary back to an hourly rate, from your hours per week and weeks per year. Amounts are before tax.
  • Image compressor
    Make a photo smaller by saving it again as WebP or JPG with a lower quality or size. The tool works in your browser and does not send your photo.
  • Compress image to KB
    Shrink a photo to fit a file size limit such as 20 KB, 50 KB or 100 KB for upload forms. The tool finds the highest quality that fits, works in your browser and does not send your photo.
  • PDF merge and split
    Join several PDF files into one, or save selected pages as a new PDF. The tool works in your browser and does not send your files.
  • QR code generator
    Turn a link or text into a QR code and save it as PNG or SVG. The tool works in your browser and does not send what you type.
  • YAML ↔ JSON converter
    Convert YAML to JSON or JSON to YAML in your browser and copy the result. YAML errors show their line number. The tool does not send the text you enter.
  • USB error guide
    What "You need to format the disk", RAW, write-protected, 0 bytes and a drive that isn't recognized mean on a USB drive or SD card, what to try first, and when recovery software can or can't help.
  • USB capacity calculator
    Enter the capacity on the package and see what Windows will show. Explains the 1000 vs 1024 difference, file system overhead and how to check a drive for fake capacity.
