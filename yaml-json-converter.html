YAML to JSON converter (and JSON to YAML)

Paste YAML or JSON, then choose the direction.

Good to know

  • YAML is read with the YAML 1.2 core schema. Dates and words such as yes or on stay as text, and merge keys (<<) are applied.
  • Custom tags (!name) and several documents separated by --- are not supported and show an error.
  • Comments are not kept. Whole numbers larger than 9007199254740991 in JSON may lose precision.

This tool does not send the text or files you enter.
